Title: Never Exhaust Your Energy Before You Even Reach The Training Hall
Author: Founder Dizfulee
Date: 2025-12-6


---

Dear martial arts practitioners,

Many of you have surely experienced this:
You head to the gym filled with excitement and overflowing energy, convinced that today will be the best training session of your life—no fatigue, no boredom, just pure focus and power.
But suddenly, the moment you step inside, a wave of fatigue hits you.
Your body feels weak, as if you’ve lost the ability to even warm up or run.
You may even feel like going back home because your motivation has vanished for no clear reason.

My dear friends,
The cause of this sudden drop in energy is one thing only:
You are unknowingly performing intense mental training before you even reach the gym.

You replay every technique in your mind…
You plan how you will train…
Which strikes you’ll execute…
Who you’ll spar with…

And how easily you’ll defeat them.
But the brain considers all these visualizations a complete training session in itself.
So it consumes a huge amount of energy before you have even begun your real practice.
By the time you enter the gym, both your mind and body have already “trained,”
which makes them unable to repeat the same effort again.
So your brain sends signals to stop, and as a result you:

Feel intense fatigue
Lose the quality of your training session
Deliver your worst performance
Gain no progress or benefit
From today onward, make sure you do not engage in mental training on your way to the gym.

Let your mind rest, so that when you step into the training arena, your true power can explode at the right moment.
Then you will witness how much stronger, more focused, and more effective your techniques and sparring will become.
Wishing you continuous progress and success.

Founder Dizfu Lee 
Creator and Founder of the International Martial Arts Style (Clever’s Kung Fu)
President of the World Martial Arts Organization (Clever’s Kung Fu)
